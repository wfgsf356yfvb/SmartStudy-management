📚 School Workflows

See the operational workflows currently supported by PlaySchool.

For school administrators

Manage school users, fee structures, announcements, reports, staff attendance, and payment submissions within the school tenant.

For teachers

Record attendance, assign homework, review submissions, grade work, publish announcements, and maintain weekly student reports.

For students

View homework, submit work, follow progress, check attendance, review fee information, and upload supporting files through protected workflows.

For operators

Super Admin users can manage schools and admins, start tenant provisioning jobs, monitor status, retry failed jobs, and review payment submissions.
